Here you will find some of my writings that I believe might be useful for other students.

- [Quantum Mechanics and Path Integrals (PDF)](/assets/pdf/path.pdf) This is a note on quantum mechanics and path integrals that I think might be more pedagogical than many found on the internet.  In particular, I start by "deriving" the configuration-space path integral schematically so that the intuition is not obstructed with math.
- [Classical Mechanics (PDF)](/assets/pdf/path.pdf) This is a note on classical mechanics.  Due to the wealth of literature on classical mechanics, these notes likely don't contribute anything new to the internet but they summarize the three main formulations of mechanics and discuss the symplectic mathematical structure underlying the Hamiltonian formulation.
